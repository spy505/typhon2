PROCEDURE: NMAP SCANNING IN KALI LINUX
STEP 1: Verify Nmap is installed in Kali Linux
nmap --version
________________________________________
STEP 2: BASIC SCAN
nmap 192.168.1.1
Purpose: Performs a basic scan on the target system
________________________________________
STEP 3: PING SCAN (HOST DISCOVERY)
nmap -sn 192.168.1.0/24
Purpose: Checks which hosts are alive (no port scanning)
________________________________________
STEP 4: TCP PORT SCAN
nmap 192.168.1.10
Purpose: Finds open TCP ports
________________________________________
STEP 5: TCP SYN SCAN (STEALTH SCAN)
sudo nmap -sS 192.168.1.10
Purpose: Performs stealth scan without completing full connection
________________________________________
STEP 6: TCP CONNECT SCAN
nmap -sT 192.168.1.10
Purpose: Full TCP connection scan (used when SYN scan is restricted)
________________________________________
STEP 7: UDP PORT SCAN
sudo nmap -sU 192.168.1.10
Purpose: Finds open UDP ports
________________________________________
STEP 8: SCAN SPECIFIC PORTS
nmap -p 22,80,443 192.168.1.10
PORT RANGE SCAN
nmap -p 1-1000 192.168.1.10
________________________________________
STEP 9: SERVICE AND VERSION DETECTION
nmap -sV 192.168.1.10
Purpose: Identifies service name and version
________________________________________
STEP 10: OS DETECTION (FINGERPRINTING)
sudo nmap -O 192.168.1.10
Purpose: Detects operating system of target
________________________________________
STEP 11: AGGRESSIVE SCAN
sudo nmap -A 192.168.1.10
Purpose: Enables OS detection, version detection, script scanning, and traceroute
________________________________________
STEP 12: SCAN MULTIPLE TARGETS
nmap 192.168.1.10 192.168.1.120
IP RANGE SCAN
nmap 192.168.1.10-50
